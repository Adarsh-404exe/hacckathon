Dengue Fever (Breakbone Fever)

Key Symptoms: High sudden fever, pain behind eyes, severe joint/muscle ache, rapid platelet drop, skin rash.

🦠 Seasonal Peak | Viral Flu

Influenza A/H3N2 & Viral Throat Infection

Key Symptoms: Persistent dry cough, running nose, sore throat, chills, fatigue, mild breathlessness.

Action Plan: Warm saline gargles, steam inhalation, paracetamol for fever, wear mask in crowded public places.
💧 Water-Borne Alert

Typhoid & Acute Gastroenteritis

Key Symptoms: Step-ladder prolonged fever, abdominal stomach cramps, loose stools, nausea, vomiting.

Action Plan: Drink boiled/purified water only, eat fresh warm home food, avoid roadside juices and raw cut salads.
